# Exelberg v011 — in-game test guide (two tracks)

Install only after explicit authorization:
`.venv/bin/python scripts/install_full_track_v011.py forward --install` and/or `... reverse --install`
(adds content/tracks/exelberg_full_v011 and exelberg_reverse_v011; older versions untouched).

## Exelberg Reverse v011 (new: Neuwaldegg -> Königstetten, ~15.77 km)
1. Spawn: Neuwaldegger Straße, westbound side, right-hand lane, ~12 m before the zebra crossing, facing the
   roundabout (as in the start screenshot). Signs at the zebra: pedestrian + bicycle crossing, end of short-term
   parking zone (+ text plate) on both sides; yield sign on the right at the ring entry; island with shrubs and a
   blue pass-right sign.
2. Roundabout counter-clockwise, 1st exit = Exelbergstraße: zebra, splitter island on the left with keep-right sign,
   red/white marker and the "Tulln →" board; deer warning on the right a bit further up.
3. Then the whole road backwards to Königstetten. Curve chevron boards face you on the outer side of each bend.
   Other signs (speed limits, warnings) are the forward-direction ones, seen from behind.
4. FINISH board / timing gate 110 m before the Königstetten end, STOP board, end wall — brake after the finish.
   Please report whether the timer starts/stops correctly.

## Exelberg Full v011 (forward, as v010)
5. Chevron boards at ~1.1 km and ~2.3 km (below Dopplerhütte) now on the outer side of the bends.
6. Roundabout: same new zebras, signs and green island; the finish and road are unchanged from v010.

Approximations: sign drawings simplified; positions from OpenStreetMap + screenshots; roundabout arms, kerbs and
signs are visual only (invisible ±6 m walls bound the drivable road); two nominal 3 m lanes in the city section.
