# Exelberg Full v006 — in-game test guide

Install only after explicit authorization: `.venv/bin/python scripts/install_full_track_v006.py --install`
(adds content/tracks/exelberg_full_v006; v001–v005 folders untouched). Track name: "Exelberg Full v006 (buildings)".

Check while driving Königstetten -> Marswiese:
1. Track loads (main KN5 is named exelberg_full_v006.kn5).
2. Signs stand closer to the asphalt, just outside the white posts; not reachable by the car.
3. Town signs (Königstetten end sign at the start, Scheiblingstein ~7.86 km / end ~8.24 km): black name on white, blue frame.
4. Red curve boards (e.g. ~0.55 km, ~1.52 km, ~2.95 km): white arrow on red.
5. Buildings at Königstetten (start), Scheiblingstein (~7.8–8.2 km), Sofienalpe (~11 km), Marswiese (~13.5–15 km):
   standing on the ground (no floating / buried houses), no buildings on or touching the road, no visible popping
   closer than ~1.5 km. Buildings have no collision.
6. Frame rate compared with v005.
Report anything odd with an approximate km or a screenshot.
